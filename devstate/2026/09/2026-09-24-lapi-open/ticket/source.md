The Traefik plugin constructor (plugin.go openOwnedLeg) must choose lapi.OpenStream or lapi.OpenLive from LapiMode. From a consumer point of view that split makes no sense. OpenStream and OpenLive do the same reclaim Open (decision store, OwnershipKey, New, hooks, bind identity). AppSec and captcha already expose a single Open. Desired: one lapi.Open that plugin.go calls the same way it calls appsec.Open and captcha.Open. The stream-collision log stays inside lapi.
