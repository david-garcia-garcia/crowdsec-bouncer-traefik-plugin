Downloads github.com/dragonflydb/dragonfly contrib/docker/docker-compose.yml and `docker compose up -d`.

Example `docker ps` line: docker.dragonflydb.io/dragonflydb/dragonfly … 0.0.0.0:6379->6379/tcp.

Responds to http and redis out of the box. redis-cli to localhost:6379. SET/GET hello world example.
