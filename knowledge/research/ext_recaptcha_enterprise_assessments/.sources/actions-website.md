Action names are specified in the `action` parameter of `grecaptcha.enterprise.execute()`.

Action names are not case-sensitive. They can only contain alphanumeric characters, slashes, and underscores.

Recommended names include `login`, `signup`, `password_reset`. Last updated 2026-09-18 UTC on fetch.
