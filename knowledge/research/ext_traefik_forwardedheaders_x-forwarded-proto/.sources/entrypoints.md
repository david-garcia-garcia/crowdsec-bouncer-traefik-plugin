forwardedHeaders.insecure: always trust forwarded headers information (X-Forwarded-*). Recommended only for tests, not production. Default false.

forwardedHeaders.trustedIPs: IPs or CIDR from where Traefik trusts the forwarded headers information (X-Forwarded-*).
