De-facto standard request header for the protocol a client used to connect to a proxy or load balancer (http or https).

Syntax: X-Forwarded-Proto: <protocol>. Example: X-Forwarded-Proto: https.

Not part of any current specification. Standardized sibling is Forwarded.

Non-standard vendor forms exist (Front-End-Https, X-Forwarded-Protocol, X-Forwarded-Ssl, X-Url-Scheme) and are not this header.
